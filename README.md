LANDSAFE AI

Landsafe AI is an AI-powered landslide early warning and risk monitoring platform. It analyzes environmental factors such as rainfall, soil moisture, slope, elevation, and historical landslide data to predict landslide risk levels.

The platform provides real-time risk mapping, location-based alerts, safe routes, shelter information, and emergency guidance for citizens. It also includes a separate authority dashboard for monitoring high-risk areas, managing alerts, and supporting faster disaster response.

Key Features:

AI-based landslide risk prediction
Live risk map
Low, Medium, and High risk classification
Location-based alerts
Explainable AI risk analysis
Safe route and shelter finder
Community incident reporting
Separate citizen and authority dashboards
Smart alert prioritization
Offline emergency information

Goal: Predict early, alert faster, and protect lives.
